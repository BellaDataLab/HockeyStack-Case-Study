# SaaS Operations Modeling: Revenue Capacity & Recruiting Optimization

## 📊 Project Summary
This project analyzes how a fast-growing SaaS company can align its **sales capacity planning** and **recruiting operations** to meet ambitious revenue and hiring targets.  
It demonstrates how quantitative modeling and scenario analysis inform strategic decisions in operations and finance.

---

## 🧠 Problem Statements

### 1️⃣ Revenue Capacity Planning
**Problem:**  
A SaaS company targets **$30M in new ARR** for the next fiscal year. Each Account Executive (AE) carries a **$1.2M annual quota** and requires a **3-month ramp** before full productivity. Leadership needs to understand:
- How many AEs to hire under different execution assumptions (quota attainment, attrition, seasonality).
- How hiring timing and productivity variability impact the capacity required to achieve the target.

**Goal:**  
Develop a financial model that quantifies required headcount across multiple execution scenarios (Upside, Base, Downside) and provides actionable hiring insights.

---

### 2️⃣ Recruiting Team Design
**Problem:**  
The company plans to hire **50 new employees** next year (30 technical, 20 non-technical) but currently has no recruiting infrastructure. Leadership must determine:
- How many recruiters are required.
- Whether additional support roles (e.g., sourcer, coordinator) are needed.
- How recruiting metrics (time-to-fill, acceptance rate, attrition) affect hiring capacity.

**Goal:**  
Construct a data-driven recruiting capacity model that aligns resources, timelines, and efficiency metrics with growth objectives.

---

## ⚙️ Approach & Methodology

### Financial Model
- Modeled AE productivity using **quota, ramp time, and attainment rates (70–90%)**.
- Incorporated **attrition (10–20%)**, **seasonality**, and **hiring timing**.
- Built three scenarios (Upside, Base, Downside) to capture execution risk.
- Validated realism using workload benchmarks (**7–14 deals per AE annually**).

### Recruiting Model
- Estimated recruiter throughput: **8–12 technical hires** and **12–18 non-technical hires** per year.
- Modeled **time-to-fill (35–70 days)** as the main driver of recruiter capacity.
- Evaluated supporting roles (sourcer, coordinator, HRBP) based on cost, efficiency, and quality of hire.
- Recommended optimal team composition for each market scenario.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### Sales Capacity
| Scenario | Attainment | Attrition | AEs Required | Key Action |
|-----------|-------------|------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Upside** | 90% | 10% | **41 AEs** | Maintain high attainment and early hiring |
| **Base** | 80% | 15% | **59 AEs** | Front-load hiring and track ramp efficiency |
| **Downside** | 70% | 20% | **89 AEs** | Increase buffer capacity and retention support |

> **Recommendation:** Front-load AE hiring in Q1 and maintain a small capacity buffer to hedge execution risk.

### Recruiting Team
| Scenario | Recruiters | Support Roles | Time-to-Fill | Strategy |
|-----------|-------------|----------------|---------------|-----------|
| **Upside** | 4 | Coordinator | 55/35 days | Lean, efficient hiring cycle |
| **Base** | 5 | Sourcer + Coordinator | 60/40 days | Balanced throughput and cost |
| **Downside** | 6 | Sourcer + Branding + HRBP | 70/50 days | Stabilize pipeline and retention |

> **Recommendation:** Anchor on 5 recruiters + 1 sourcer + 1 coordinator, adjusting based on time-to-fill and acceptance rates.
